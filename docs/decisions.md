# Decisions

One dated line per decision that departs from the kit or closes an open question.

- 2026-11-05 — Scaffolded from hackathon-foundation starter.
- 2026-11-06 — Theme is dark riso (pink/cyan) instead of the kit's light default: owner picked the Playmat TCG look. Landing lives in Framer; the app in `apps/web` carries the same tokens.
- 2026-11-06 — Marketplace = per-match pick board (pari-mutuel "×N if he wins"). Secondary trading of cards is out of scope.
- 2026-11-06 — Framer landing is marketing only; the full flow (board, market, pack opening, binder, claim) is built in the app, spec in docs/ux-flow.md.
- 2026-11-06 — Secondary market for sealed cards: visible pick, per-player cap (10 per player per match), pre-kickoff only, 5% sale burn; opened cards can never be sold. Supersedes the earlier "secondary trading out of scope" line.
- 2026-11-06 — Random card packs (Silver/Gold/Diamond, stake-weighted payout, reveal on open) go to the roadmap; not built before submission because they change the payout core.
- 2026-11-06 — App structure: `/` is a landing hero with no navbar (a live showcase of the three card states: no card, sealed, opened) and one Launch App button; Launch App goes to a dedicated Connect Wallet page; after connect, the nav is Matches, Binder, Leaderboard, with Rules and Sound as icon buttons top right.
- 2026-11-06 — UI pass: the Rules icon leaves the masthead; Matches keeps the Framer Board exactly (heading, intro, Discipline Key, Settled archive) and has no filters or flipping deck; fixture cards share a row height; the Next Kickoff card fills its column; the Binder content aligns to the same top edge as other pages; Market drops the Action Panel for Pick a Player and Buy Sealed buttons top right of the Team v Team row. The landing keeps the Framer board and turns the bottom dock into a single Launch App button (no masthead CTA), restoring the 609px playmat height. Landing copy now matches Framer: masthead shows London time and the next matchday, the competition tabs are gone, the Field is the static Framer Pack, the Binder carries the "How claims work" link, and labels are "How it plays" / "House rules".
- 2026-11-06 — UI/UX audit: filters and sorts now use quiet "chips" (1px Line Soft ring, Small text, Pink fill) instead of the Dock-style segmented control, so they no longer read as the navbar; the Market heading is "The squads" (the "Pick a Player" button is the only CTA); the match state track uses arrows and a badge instead of tab-like text; the Rules "This Week" note drops the card price and burn that already sit under House Rules; the Binder shelf labels are no longer clipped by the scroll. De-duplication: the Market header drops the matchday/number/competition/date meta that the sleeve already carries (only the state pill repeats), Leaderboard "Your Standing" keeps only the rank since the "You" row holds the stats, the squad sort shows a "Sort" label, and "Buy Sealed" shows the listing count. The masthead Sound icon is dropped (the pack still carries its own toggle), and since a wallet picks a player only once, the header keeps the Pick a Player / Buy Sealed buttons; the pick shows only on its squad tile (the pink "Your Pick" badge), not in a header panel. The "How settling works"/"How claims work" legend zones stack the card-backs above the copy (they were squeezed into a ~94px column beside the icon, so the link text overflowed the zone), and the hero "Launch App" button gains a rocket icon.
- 2026-11-06 — Dedicated Connect page removed (supersedes the earlier "Launch App goes to a dedicated Connect Wallet page" line): Launch App goes straight to Matches; Matches, Market, Leaderboard and Rules browse without a wallet; the Binder, Pack and Ops show an inline connect gate, and every write (pick/mint, buy, sell, open, claim, refund, finalize) asks to connect in place before it signs.
- 2026-11-06 — Deployments: `packages/config/src/deployments.json` holds a `mainnet` and a `local` record (addresses, deploy block, fixture slug to match id), written by `scripts/deploy.ts`; fixtures no longer carry a matchId. A warm-up is listed with a kickoff a few minutes ahead (listMatch needs a future kickoff) and the app shows its real date.
- 2026-11-06 — Copy pass: one short line per block, explanatory paragraphs cut (Market drops the "How this match settles" phases, Leaderboard drops "How It Ranks"). Leaderboard is a podium of the top three as Result cards (1st Pink print raised in the middle, 2nd cyan, 3rd Note badges like the Discipline Key) with the rest in one Card panel; the HTML table is gone.
- 2026-11-06 — Binder actions live on the back of the card: a sealed pack flips to Open Your Pack and Sell, an opened card flips to its result with Claim or Refund. Under every card sits only Open Match.
- 2026-11-06 — Generated art: player cards show a faceless cut-out figure (team kit, a pose per position) over the app's own team halftone with a riso offset print and the number in the corner; match covers are floodlit flare scenes in both sides' colours with the house halftone print. Images come from scripts/gen-art.ts (Bedrock) via CloudFront; anything without art keeps the drawn SVG.
- 2026-11-07 — Contract: cards move only through `buy` (`transferFrom` reverts `CardLocked`), and an uncalled pot with no open next match is burned instead of going to the owner. `cardOf` stays the minter's slot by design.
- 2026-11-07 — Locked: 1,000 $WIF per card, 10% pot burn, 5% sale burn, 2h dispute, cap 10 per player. Settlement = highest FotMob player rating with the FotMob match URL (tie splits; SofaScore fallback), posted by the settler key; no provider API.
